Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Offlist keeps things simple. We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Here's exactly what we do use.

1. Local storage (not a tracking cookie)

When you use the interactive demo, the checklists, employees, and templates you create are saved using your browser's local storage — a standard web technology, not a cookie. This data:

  • Stays only on your device, in your browser
  • Is never transmitted to Offlist or any third party
  • Can be cleared at any time from your browser's settings, or with the "Reset demo data" button inside the demo

2. Cookie consent preference

We store one small piece of local storage to remember whether you've accepted or declined our cookie banner, so we don't show it to you again on every visit. We also remember your light/dark mode preference the same way.

3. What we don't use

  • No advertising cookies
  • No third-party analytics trackers, at this time
  • No cross-site tracking of any kind

If this changes in the future, we'll update this policy and our cookie banner accordingly.

4. Third-party services

Our contact form is submitted through a third-party form-delivery service so your message reaches our inbox. That service may set its own cookies while the form page is open; this is governed by that provider's own policy, not ours.

5. Managing local storage

You can clear all locally stored Offlist data at any time via your browser's site settings (usually under "Privacy" or "Site data"), or by clearing local storage for this site directly.

6. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach out via our contact page.
