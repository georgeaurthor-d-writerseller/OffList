HOW IT WORKS

A departure has a shape. Offlist follows it.

Every departure moves through the same days, whether it's planned or immediate.

Departure logged, checklist auto-created for IT + HR

WITHOUT IT VS. WITH IT

What actually changes

🟠 Without Offlist

  • IT finds out about a departure the morning of, or after
  • Access is revoked "as remembered," not as a list
  • Equipment return depends on someone following up manually
  • No record of what was actually done for a former employee

🟢 With Offlist

  • A checklist is created the moment a departure is logged
  • Every system to revoke is listed — nothing relies on memory
  • IT and HR see the same list, split by who owns what
  • A timestamped record exists for every completed departure
QUESTIONS

Everything worth knowing before you start

What is Offlist?

Offlist is a shared checklist tool that makes sure every step of an employee's offboarding — access revocation, equipment return, HR paperwork — actually gets done and gets recorded.

Who is it built for?

IT teams and HR/People Ops teams who currently split offboarding across email, memory, and separate spreadsheets — plus agencies and MSPs who manage offboarding for multiple client organizations.

Can I try it before talking to anyone?

Yes — the live demo is open to everyone, no account needed. Test it with sample departures right now.

Why do I need to contact you instead of just signing up?

We set up every paid workspace personally so your checklist templates and team roles are configured correctly from day one, rather than leaving you to figure it out alone. It takes one short conversation.

Can I customize the checklist items?

Yes. Templates are fully editable — add, remove, or reorder items per category, and every new departure picks up your current template.

Does it integrate with our other tools?

Offlist is currently a standalone checklist and audit trail. Direct integrations with identity providers and ticketing tools are on our roadmap — ask us about your specific stack.
