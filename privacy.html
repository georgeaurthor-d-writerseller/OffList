Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information Offlist collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. It applies to our marketing site, our interactive demo, and — once available — our hosted application.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Offlist, we may collect:

  • Contact information you provide directly, such as your name and email address when you message us through our contact form.
  • Demo data you enter while testing the interactive demo. This data is stored only in your own browser's local storage and is never transmitted to us.
  • Account and workspace data, once a paid workspace is available — such as employee names, departure dates, and checklist records your organization enters to use the product.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and general usage patterns, if we add analytics in the future. We will update this policy before doing so.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to messages sent through our contact form
  • To operate and improve the product
  • To communicate about your account or workspace, once accounts exist
  • To meet legal and security obligations

3. What we don't do

We do not sell your information. We do not use employee or departure data entered into the product for advertising purposes.

4. Data storage and security

Demo data lives entirely in your browser's local storage and never reaches our servers. Once a hosted paid product is available, workspace data will be stored on secured infrastructure with access limited to what's needed to operate the service.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage for demo data and to remember your cookie preferences. We do not currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details.

6. Third-party services

Our contact form is processed through a third-party form service to deliver your message to us by email. That provider processes the name, email, and message you submit solely to deliver it; we don't share this with anyone else.

7. Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion, by reaching out through our contact page. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under laws such as GDPR or CCPA.

8. Children's privacy

Offlist is a business tool and is not directed at, or intended for use by, children under 16.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach out via our contact page.
