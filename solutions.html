For IT teams

Access left open after someone leaves is one of the most preventable security gaps — and one of the most common. Offlist gives IT a single, unambiguous list per departure.

  • Every system to revoke, listed per departure — not left to memory
  • A timestamped log of exactly what was revoked and when
  • Shared visibility with HR, without giving them access to admin consoles
